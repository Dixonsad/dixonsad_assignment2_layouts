Annual Fall Hackathon

Join 500+ students for our biggest tech event of the year. Build innovative software, meet industry mentors, and compete for over $10,000 in prizes!

About the Event

The Annual Fall Hackathon is a 48-hour invention marathon where students come together to build creative software and hardware projects. Whether you're a seasoned developer or writing your first line of code, there is a place for you here.

What to Expect

Attendees can expect a fast-paced environment fueled by free food, caffeine, and collaboration. Mentors from top tech companies will be roaming the floor to help you debug and brainstorm.

Agenda

  • Friday 5:00 PM: Registration & Team Formation
  • Friday 7:00 PM: Opening Ceremony & Hacking Begins
  • Saturday 12:00 PM: Workshop: Intro to APIs
  • Sunday 12:00 PM: Hacking Ends, Judging Begins
  • Sunday 3:00 PM: Awards Ceremony

Accessibility & Participation

The Engineering Building is fully wheelchair accessible. Quiet rooms will be available on the second floor. If you require ASL interpretation or have dietary restrictions, please note them on your registration form.

Event Details

  • Date: Sept 25 - Sept 27, 2026
  • Time: Starts at 5:00 PM
  • Location: Engineering Building, Room 101
  • Organizer: UNCG Computer Science Club
  • Admission: Free for UNCG Students
