About the Student Engagement Office

Our mission is to connect students with experiences that enrich their time at UNCG. Whether you are looking for professional development, entertainment, or community service, we have something for you.

By participating in campus activities, you gain access to:

  • Networking opportunities with alumni and industry professionals.
  • Leadership development and resume-building experiences.
  • A vibrant, diverse community and lifelong friendships.
